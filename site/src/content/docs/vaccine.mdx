## Hepatitis B Vaccine

- At birth if infant’s birth weight ≥&nbsp;2,000g  
- At 30 days of life if infant’s birth weight &lt;&nbsp;2,000g  
  *Give at 30 days of life even if the infant has not reached 2 kg.*

## 2-month vaccine

- Pediarix (dTaP, HepB, Polio)
- PCV
- HiB
